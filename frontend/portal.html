Hybrid Architecture Coexistence

Architecture Contract

MySQL 8.0 (Primary Relational)

  • Authoritative source of truth for students, rooms, and billing.
  • Guarantees ACID transactions for room bookings and payments.
  • Enforces referential integrity, foreign keys, and triggers.
  • Validates vacancy before any room allocation is committed.
⇄

FalkorDB (Graph Intelligence)

  • Stores property graph projection of rooms, messes, and peers.
  • Rapid multi-hop traversals for roommate relationship networks.
  • Hotspot aggregation grouping complaints to specific facilities.
  • Graph hierarchy traversal (Hostel -> Block -> Floor -> Room).
